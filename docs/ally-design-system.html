Do's & don'ts

Do Treat the two purples as functionally distinct — primary for CTAs, brand-plum for structural type and panel fills.
Don't Collapse them into a single purple. It flattens the chromatic depth the deeper plum carries across 87 occurrences.
Do Keep every CTA at 24px radius — the brand's signature sub-pill action shape.
Don't Escalate to a 999px pill. That reads as a neobank (Monzo, Nubank), not an online-but-traditional bank.
Do Use lifestyle photography as the hero canvas — the 80px display needs it to read as editorial, not a shout.
Don't Introduce banking blue anywhere. Purple is the deliberate move against the legacy-bank convention.
Do Reserve brand-plum-deepest for the bottom co-brand panel only — the page's single structural climax.
Don't Render the lowercase "ally" wordmark in uppercase or at display size — the identity is voice-down by design.
